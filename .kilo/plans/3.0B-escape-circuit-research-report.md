# Fly Brain 3.0B: Drosophila Escape/Startle Circuit Research Report

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Status:** Research phase — pre-implementation
**Sources:** FlyWire/Codex (FAFB whole-brain), MANC (Male Adult Nerve Cord), Hemibrain, published literature (Ache et al. 2019, von Reyn et al. 2014, Card & Dickinson 2008, Allen et al. 2006, King & Wyman 1980, Tanouye & Wyman 1980, Cheong et al. 2026 eLife, Dombrovski et al. 2023/2025, Kennedy & Broadie 2018, Azevedo et al. 2024)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Drosophila escape/startle pathway is the best-characterized rapid sensorimotor circuit in the fly. It comprises:
- **Visual detection**: LC4 (velocity/η detector) and LPLC2 (angular size/ρ detector) visual projection neurons (VPNs) in optic glomeruli
- **Descending command**: Giant Fiber (GF, aka DNp01) — a pair of large-axon descending neurons
- **VNC premotor**: Direct electrical + chemical synapses onto TTMn (jump) and PSI→DLMn (flight initiation)
- **Modulation**: GFC1-4 electrically coupled neurons, VNC intrinsic interneurons (IN05B032, IN21A032, IN00A062, IN05B072), ascending neurons (AN08B098), GABAergic gate (AN05B006)
- **Motor output**: TTMn → tergotrochanteral muscle (TTM, jump); DLMn → dorsal longitudinal muscle (DLM, wing depressor); DVMn → dorsal ventral muscle (wing levator); steering muscles

Two behavioral modes:
- **Short-mode** ("tuck and jump"): GF-driven, ~10-15 ms latency, unstable flight
- **Long-mode**: Wing raise → jump → downstroke, GF-dispensable, stable flight

This report compiles quantitative connectomic data for implementing a minimal, defensible 3.0B biological circuit.

## 5. Timing & Latency Budget (Short-Mode Escape)

| Stage | Latency (ms) | Cumulative |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| Visual stimulus → LC4/LPLC2 response | ~10-20 (phototransduction + VPN) | 10-20 |
| VPN → GF synaptic integration | ~2-5 | 12-25 |
| GF spike initiation → axon conduction (brain→VNC) | ~2-4 | 14-29 |
| GF → TTMn (electrical) | ~1-2 | 15-31 |
| TTMn → TTM muscle (jump) | ~1-2 (EJPs) | 16-33 |
| GF → PSI (electrical) | ~1-2 | 15-31 |
| PSI → DLMn (chemical) | ~2-3 | 17-34 |
| DLMn → DLM (wing downstroke) | ~1-2 | 18-36 |

**Total short-mode latency: ~15-35 ms** (matches von Reyn et al. 2014: "single GF action potential triggers takeoff in <10 ms from GF spike")

## 7. Minimal Defensible 3.0B Circuit (Proposed)

### 7.1 Core Pathway (Must Implement)

```
Looming Stimulus
      │
      ├─→ LC4 (velocity/η) ──┐
      │                       ├─→ GF (summation: w₁·η + w₂·ρ) ──→ TTMn (electrical) ──→ Jump
      └─→ LPLC2 (size/ρ) ────┘                            │
                                                         └─→ PSI (electrical) ──→ DLMn (chemical) ──→ Wing Downstroke
```

### 7.2 Modulatory Layer (Should Implement)

```
VNC Intrinsic:
  IN05B032, IN21A032, IN00A062 ──| GF terminal (inhibition)
  IN05B072 ──────────────────────→ IN05B032 (disinhibition)
  AN08B098 ──────────────────────→ GF axon (recurrent excitation)
  AN05B006 ─────────────────────| GF axon + AN19B001/AN18B001 (broad inhibition)

Long-Mode (GF-independent):
  DNp06/11/02/04 ──→ AN19B001_rostral/caudal, AN18B001 ──→ Wing raise (DVMn, tp, b) → Jump (TTMn) → Downstroke (DLMn)
```

### 7.3 Sensory Feedback (Nice to Have)

```
Wing campaniform SNs ──→ IN13A022 ──→ TTMn, GFC2
T2 leg SNs ───────────→ GFC2
```

## 10. Open Questions / Uncertainties for 3.0B Implementation

1. **Exact GF resting potential in vivo** — reported -65mV but varies; use -65mV as baseline
2. **LC4/LPLC2 relative weights (w₁, w₂)** — Ache 2019 model fits w₁≈w₂ but exact ratio unknown; start equal
3. **GFC2 direct vs indirect functional distinction** — connectomic only; no physiology yet
4. **AN05B006 GABA confirmation** — prediction probability <0.7; EASI-FISH pending
5. **Long-mode DN identities** — DNp06/11/02/04 implicated but not exhaustive
6. **Sensory feedback timing** — campaniform→IN13A022→TTMn latency unknown
7. **Bilateral coordination** — GF is bilateral pair; do they spike synchronously? (Evidence: yes, dye-coupled)
